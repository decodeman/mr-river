Maybe we should know each other

I keep thinking there ought to be a better way for unusually compatible people to find each other before coincidence does all the work.

So this is mine.

Maybe you’re a woman and there’s chemistry. Maybe you’re a guy who thinks we should build something together. Maybe we become friends, collaborators, pen pals, co-conspirators on an idea, or people who send each other things at 11:47 p.m. because you are the one person who will understand why this is interesting.

I’m trying to make it easier for the right people to recognize each other.

About this profile

I gave my AI an absurd amount of information about me and asked it to help make me legible. This page is its synthesis. If it gets too full of itself, blame the machine. :)

The AI version

Mr. River is a software architect, musician, lifelong athlete, father, systems thinker, and relentlessly curious person in the Kansas City area.

He tends to get interested in the layer underneath the obvious layer: relationships, consciousness, giftedness, childhood, technology, AI, faith, human flourishing, game design, psychology, and whatever unexpected thing catches fire next.

He likes people who bring a real inner world with them. People who notice things. People who have theories. People who can get very serious and then say something ridiculous five seconds later.

What tends to create the spark

Curiosity with momentum.

Warmth. Playfulness. Original thought. Emotional depth that stays connected to life. People who get fascinated and pull someone else into the fascination. People who can disagree, revise, laugh, build, wonder, and keep going.

He likes conversations that generate more conversation. He likes ideas that become experiments, decisions, projects, better questions, or different ways of living.

He also likes range. One hour might be consciousness, relationships, AI, or what makes a good childhood. The next might be tennis, music, a dumb joke, food, a lake, or trying to figure out why something everybody accepts makes absolutely no sense.

Build something, think about something, become friends

Some people are interesting because of what they know. Others because of what happens when two minds start interacting.

Mr. River is especially interested in the second kind.

He enjoys building systems, products, research approaches, games, models, and strange little experiments. He is drawn to people who have their own obsessions and who enjoy letting ideas collide.

A good outcome from this page could be a friendship, a collaboration, a long-running correspondence, a business idea, a creative partnership, an introduction to someone else, or simply meeting a person who makes the world feel more interesting.

People, not networking

He likes actual friendship more than networking.

That means conversation with some texture to it. Telling each other what we are really working on. Sending the article, half-formed theory, song, question, joke, or idea that made us think of the other person.

He tends to prefer one-on-one connection and has plenty of appetite for depth, but he also likes lightness. Wit is good. Cheesiness is allowed. A joke can miss completely and still be worth making if at least one person is amused. He is frequently willing to be that one person.

Mr. River is looking for a serious romantic partnership, and unusual friendship or intellectual resonance can sometimes be part of how attraction begins. He is drawn to warmth, affection, playfulness, curiosity, physical aliveness, and a woman who genuinely enjoys substantial everyday closeness.

Ordinary life still counts

Music in the house. Exercise. Kids. Friends. Working from home. A long conversation. Something outside. Making something. Laughing. Quiet. Getting deeply interested in a subject for no commercially defensible reason whatsoever.

A lot of what he wants from people is simply the feeling that both of us become a little more alive, more curious, or more ourselves through knowing each other.
